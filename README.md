# Traitor Trivia

**Answer the questions. Spot the traitor. Trust nobody.**

Traitor Trivia is a party game that combines trivia, deception, and social deduction. Players must navigate trivia challenges while trying to figure out who they can trust.

The challenge isn't just knowing the answers — it's working out who is playing honestly and who might be secretly working against everyone else.

🎮 **Play here:** https://traitor-trivia.netlify.app/

## 🎯 Objective

Complete the trivia challenges, pay attention to suspicious behaviour, and identify the traitor before it's too late.

Players must use their knowledge, judgement, and ability to read others to work out who is telling the truth.

## 📜 Rules

- **Follow the round instructions:** Each round presents its own challenge and objectives.
- **Answer carefully:** Use your knowledge to answer trivia questions and make informed decisions.
- **Stay observant:** Pay attention to other players' answers and behaviour.
- **Question suspicious actions:** Not everything is necessarily as it seems.
- **Identify the traitor:** Work together to figure out who might be sabotaging the group's efforts.
- **Play strategically:** Decide when to share information, who to trust, and how to approach each challenge.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game and follow the on-screen instructions to set up a session.
2. Read the rules and understand your role and objective.
3. Play through the trivia rounds and complete the challenges.
4. Watch how others behave and discuss anything suspicious.
5. Make your decisions based on the information available.
6. Follow the game's final-round instructions to determine the outcome.

## 🏆 The Challenge

Traitor Trivia combines three key elements:

- 🧠 **Knowledge** — Answer trivia questions and demonstrate what you know.
- 🕵️ **Deduction** — Analyse the information available and look for inconsistencies.
- 🎭 **Deception** — Decide who deserves your trust and who might be hiding something.
